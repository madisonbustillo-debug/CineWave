Behind the Frame

More than edits, I build visual stories.

I am Louie Jay C. Dalo, a first-year BSIT student majoring in Multimedia Arts and Animation. I work as a video editor, photographer, lyric video editor, videographer, and graphic designer.

My creative style focuses on emotion, timing, lighting, and clean visual presentation. Whether I am editing a video, capturing an event, or designing a layout, I always try to make the output feel meaningful and visually engaging.
