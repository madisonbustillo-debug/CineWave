About CineWave

We are a five-member freelance team combining media, design, art, and development.

Our Identity

Our team brings together different strengths: video editing, cinematography, photography, development, graphic design, oil painting, and UI/UX interest. Instead of presenting ourselves separately, this website acts as one creative studio where clients can understand what we offer and explore each member’s personal portfolio.
